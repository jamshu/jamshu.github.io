My Sithu,

Nine years ago you were nineteen, and somehow we began with arguments before we ever began with love. Then you loved me madly — and yes, we still fought over the smallest things. I wouldn't trade a single one of those quarrels, because every one of them was ours.

Today there are miles between us — me here in Kingdom, you back home with our two daughters. The distance is hard. But distance has never been able to touch what I feel for you. You are still the girl I argued with, still the woman who loved me without holding back.

Thank you for holding our home together while I'm away, for being mother and anchor to our girls. On your 28th birthday I wish I could be there to see you smile. Until I am, know that you are loved across every mile.

Happy birthday, my love. Always yours.
